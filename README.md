# AURA Spa Portal

Build a luxury Medical Spa website for "AURA | Medical Aesthetics & Spa" with React, Tailwind CSS, and Lucide icons.

Design & Style:
- Palette: Soft warm beige/cream background (#FAF8F5), deep muted slate/charcoal text (#1A1A1A), subtle warm gold accent (#C5A059), and muted sage green elements.
- Typography: Elegant serif headers (Playfair Display / Cormorant style) paired with clean sans-serif body text (Inter / Plus Jakarta Sans).
- Editorial luxury layout with generous whitespace, smooth scroll transitions, and refined card hover effects.

Key Sections:
1. Header: Logo "AURA | Medical Aesthetics & Spa", nav links (Treatments, About, Philosophy, Contact), and sticky gold CTA "Book Consultation".
2. Hero: "Elevate Your Natural Beauty with Clinical Excellence", bespoke subheadline, "Explore Treatments" button, and "Direct Support Booking" secondary button with concierge/WhatsApp icon.
3. Treatments Grid (8 offerings in responsive cards with icons, descriptions, and "Inquire / Book" micro-actions):
   - Skin Consultation
   - Botox Treatments
   - Dermal Fillers
   - Medical-Grade Facials
   - Laser Treatment
   - Body Sculpting & Treatment
   - IV Infusion Therapy
   - Weight Management
4. Why Choose Us / Philosophy: 3 pillars (Expert Clinical Practitioners, State-of-the-Art Technology, Tailored Client Care).
5. Interactive Booking Modal & Consultation Flow for booking appointments.
6. Floating Action Widget (FAB): Bottom-right "Instant Booking & Support" opening a direct concierge chat/booking modal.
7. Footer: Minimalist luxury footer with business hours, clinic locations, social links, and contact details.
